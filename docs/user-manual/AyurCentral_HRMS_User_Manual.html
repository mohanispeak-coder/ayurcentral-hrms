1. About this app

AyurCentral HRMS is a web app you open in a browser. You sign in with your Google account. The app helps your company manage people, leave, attendance, payroll, and recruitment.

This manual matches the code in your export file (not a generic branch). It lists 0 menu items and 101 project files.

2. Sign in

  1. Open the HRMS web app link from your IT or HR team (URL ends with /exec).
  2. Sign in with your work Google email.
  3. If you see access denied, ask Admin to add your user and role.

Layout: menu on the left, your name and notifications on top, main page in the center. Use the Ask HR button for help questions when it is on screen.

                  6. HR guide

                  Employees

                  • Search and filter the directory; open a row for the full profile.
                  • Add employee - one person at a time. Fields with a red star are required.
                  • Bulk upload - import many rows from Excel or CSV template.
                  • Custom fields - add extra columns for profiles and bulk upload.
                  • Mandatory fields - choose which fields are required on create and bulk upload.
                  • Employee codes in bulk upload use format like: SAPL-0001, AOPL-0001, AOMS-0001.

                  Leave

                  Leave (admin) for company requests and leave types. Leave Approvals for pending decisions.

                  Attendance

                  Register - monthly attendance bulk upload. Form T - Form T Excel export by vertical.

                  Payroll

                  Salary structure templates, Salary Statement reports, Payroll monthly run (calculate, finalize, payslips).

                  Recruitment

                  Recruitment, Jobs, and Candidates for hiring workflow.

                  10. Help and tips

                  • Wait for the Save button to finish; do not double-click.
                  • Bulk upload errors often mean wrong employee code, duplicate email, or missing required columns.
                  • For company policy (how much leave, pay dates), follow your HR policy - this manual is only for the software.
