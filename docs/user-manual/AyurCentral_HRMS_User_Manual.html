1. About this system

AyurCentral HRMS is a web application for your company. You use it in a browser (Chrome is recommended). It helps you manage:

  • Employee records (personal details, job details, documents)
  • Leave (apply, approve, balances)
  • Attendance (register upload, Form T)
  • Payroll (salary structure, monthly payroll, payslips, salary statement)
  • Recruitment (jobs and candidates)
  • Company settings and notifications

Your company stores data in a secure Google Sheet behind the app. You do not need to open the sheet for normal work — use the HRMS website your IT team gives you.

2. Sign in and first steps

2.1 Open the app

  1. Open the HRMS link (it usually ends with /exec).
  2. Sign in with your Google work account when asked.
  3. If you see “Access denied”, contact HR or Admin. Your email must be added as a user with the correct role.

2.2 Screen layout

  • Left sidebar — main menu. Tap section names to expand (My Work, People, Time Off, etc.).
  • Top bar — your name, role, notifications bell, Sign out.
  • Main area — the page you selected.
  • Ask HR — chat help button (bottom right) when enabled.

2.3 Sign out

Click Sign out on the top right when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

3. User roles — who can do what

RoleMain purpose
EmployeeView own profile, apply for leave, download own payslips (if allowed).
ManagerSame as employee, plus see My Team and approve leave for reports.
HRManage employees, leave admin, attendance, payroll, recruitment, settings (with limits).
Admin / OwnerFull HRMS access including settings and user management areas.
Admin can turn modules on or off per role under System → Settings → HRMS module access by role. If you do not see a menu item, your role may not have that module.

5. Admin guide

Admins set up the system and control access. Work with HR for day-to-day employee data.

5.1 Settings (System → Settings)

  • Email toggles — turn leave, payroll, and welcome emails on or off.
  • HRMS module access by role — check boxes so Employee, Manager, HR, or Admin can see each area (Dashboard, Employees, Payroll, etc.).
  • New login defaults — default rights for documents, payslips, leave self-service per role.
  • Content templates — edit email text where shown.
  • Click Save at the bottom. Ask users to refresh the browser after big changes.

5.2 Notifications

View in-app messages and email log. Useful to check if emails were sent or skipped.

5.3 Users

The Users menu may show “Soon”. User roles are still managed through your user setup process (sheet / admin tools your IT uses).

5.4 Deploy and updates

Technical staff update the app with Google Apps Script (clasp push) and redeploy the web app. After updates, users should hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) once.

6. HR guide

6.1 Employees (People → Employees)

  • Search and filters — find staff by name, ID, department, location, vertical.
  • Add employee — one person at a time. Required fields show a red star (*).
  • Bulk upload — many employees from Excel/CSV template.
  • Custom fields — add extra columns for profiles and bulk upload.
  • Mandatory fields — choose which fields are required on create and bulk upload.
  • Open a row to see full profile: personal, employment, payroll, documents, payslips, access.

6.2 Mandatory fields (People → Mandatory fields)

  1. Open the checklist grouped by Identity, Work, Bank, etc.
  2. Tick Mandatory for fields that must be filled when creating employees.
  3. Employee code always stays mandatory.
  4. Click Save. The page reloads so forms and bulk templates match your choices.

6.3 Bulk employee upload

  1. Go to Bulk upload from Employees page.
  2. Download the template (new hire or legacy variant).
  3. Fill rows. Use employee codes like SAPL-0001, AOPL-0001, AOMS-0001.
  4. Upload file → review validation → confirm import.
Do not change header names in the template. Columns with * are required based on mandatory field settings.

6.4 Leave administration (Time Off → Leave)

  • Manage leave types and policies.
  • View balances and requests across the company.
  • Support employees who need balance corrections (follow your company process).

6.5 Leave approvals

HR and Managers use Leave Approvals to approve or reject pending requests.

6.6 Attendance

  • Register — download template, upload monthly attendance in bulk.
  • Form T — generate / work with Form T reports as per your payroll process.

6.7 Payroll

  • Salary structure — define pay components and structures assigned to employees.
  • Salary Statement — reports for payroll review.
  • Payroll — select month, sync employees, enter inputs, calculate, finalize, generate payslips.

Follow your company checklist: lock only when data is complete (bank details, structure, etc.) if your settings require it.

6.8 Recruitment

  • Recruitment — overview.
  • Jobs — create and edit job posts.
  • Candidates — track applicants through stages.

7. Manager guide

  • My Team — list of your direct reports; open a profile to view work details.
  • Leave Approvals — approve or reject leave for your team.
  • My Leave / My Profile / My Payslips — same as any employee.
  • Recruitment — view jobs and candidates if your Admin enabled it for Managers.

Managers cannot add company-wide employees or run payroll unless they also have an HR/Admin role.

8. Employee guide

8.1 My Profile

View your job details and personal information. You may upload some documents if your company allows. You cannot change all fields — HR updates sensitive data.

8.2 My Leave

  1. Open My Leave.
  2. Check your balance for the leave year.
  3. Click to apply for leave (dates, type, reason if needed).
  4. Wait for Manager or HR approval. You may get email if toggles are on.

8.3 My Payslips

If enabled in Settings for your role, open My Payslips, select period, and download PDF.

8.4 Notifications

Use the bell icon on the top bar for recent updates (if shown for your role).

9. Common tasks (step by step)

9.1 HR: Add one new employee

  1. People → Employees → Add employee.
  2. Fill all fields with a red star.
  3. Choose vertical (SAPL / AOPL / AOMS) and correct employee code format.
  4. Save. Create login separately if your process uses Google login for the employee.

9.2 HR: Change which fields are required

  1. People → Mandatory fields (or Employees → Mandatory fields button).
  2. Update checkboxes → Save.

9.3 Employee: Apply for leave

  1. My Work → My Leave → Apply.
  2. Pick dates and leave type → Submit.

9.4 Manager: Approve leave

  1. Time Off → Leave Approvals.
  2. Open request → Approve or Reject with comment if needed.

9.5 HR: Run monthly payroll (overview)

  1. Payroll → open the payroll month.
  2. Sync employees, enter attendance/LOP if needed.
  3. Calculate → review → finalize → generate payslips.

10. Tips, errors, and help

  • Page looks old after update — press Ctrl+Shift+R (hard refresh) once.
  • Save button spins — wait; do not click twice.
  • Validation errors — read the red text; fix fields and try again.
  • Access denied — your role or module access does not allow this; contact Admin.
  • Bulk upload fails — check employee code format, duplicate emails, and required columns (*).
For policy questions (how much leave, payroll dates), follow your company HR policy. This manual explains how to use the software only.
